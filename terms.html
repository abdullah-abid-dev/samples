Terms of Service

Last updated: August 2026. These terms outline the service offered.

The service

I provide transcription, subtitles/captions, and related content (show notes, blog posts, quote graphics, analytics) and, where requested, web pages to publish transcripts. Deliverables are produced from the source media you provide.

Accuracy & review

Every transcript is human-reviewed for accuracy. On heavily degraded audio I aim to get as close as possible and flag any genuinely inaudible passages rather than guess. One round of free revisions is included; further revisions may be billed per the agreed quote.

Confidentiality

Your files and their contents are kept confidential and are not published or shared without your explicit permission. Public sample pages are either demos I have chosen to share or, where marked, private reviewed samples accessed by password.

Payment

Pricing follows the per-minute base rate plus any selected add-ons, as quoted. Bulk and recurring work is discounted by agreement. Payment terms are confirmed in the project quote.

Limitation of liability

The service is provided for general transcription and accessibility purposes. While every effort is made for accuracy, no guarantee of 100% verbatim perfection is made on low-quality audio. Liability is limited to the value of the project.
