The most universally supported subtitle format. Pair it with your video or audio in any media player, or upload it to a platform to display captions.

In VLC, MPV or QuickTime, open your media then add the .srt as a subtitle track. On hosting platforms upload it as a caption file: YouTube Studio (Subtitles → upload), Spotify for Podcasters, Buzzsprout, Libsyn, Blubrry, Podbean and Amazon Music (video podcasts) all accept SRT.

The modern web-standard caption format used by HTML5 players and most podcast hosts.

Upload the .vtt as a caption track in YouTube, Spotify for Podcasters, Buzzsprout, Libsyn, Blubrry and Podbean. Apple Podcasts and Amazon Music also consume VTT for captioned episodes, and you can embed it directly in a player on your own site.
