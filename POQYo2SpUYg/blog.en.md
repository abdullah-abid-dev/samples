# Mishi Khan on Animal Rights, Social Responsibility, and the Power of Kindness

In this episode of *Pakistan Observer Podcast*, host Abdullah Malik sits down with activist Mishi Khan to explore why she is so passionate about animal welfare. Listeners will learn about her unique approach to advocacy, her personal journey of adopting dogs, and her critical insights into the societal mindset that often leads to animal cruelty.

## The Responsibility of the Platform

Mishi Khan believes that having a public platform comes with a moral obligation to speak on issues beyond personal interests. She observes that while social media is dominated by topics like makeup, skincare, and glamour, vital issues like animal rights often go unnoticed.

> "If Allah has given you a position and a platform where people know you, there is a responsibility to speak about other things too. You must use your platform in a constructive manner."

She argues that awareness is crucial, noting that before the digital age, people lacked the tools to amplify voices for the voiceless. Mishi emphasizes that humanity has the power to think and reason, unlike animals, and therefore bears the duty to protect them.

*   **Use your voice:** Utilize your social media presence to highlight injustices.
*   **Constructive dialogue:** Advocate for change without causing unnecessary conflict or offense.
*   **Awareness first:** Prioritize educating the public over simply entertaining them.

## The Psychology of Cruelty and Frustration

A significant portion of the conversation addresses the root causes of animal abuse in Pakistan. Mishi Khan identifies frustration as a primary driver, suggesting that society often redirects its anger onto animals and children because it lacks healthy outlets for stress.

She critiques the "mindset" of the dominant few who mock the weak, whether it is a child playing with a dog or a person sitting quietly in a meeting. This behavior is described as insidious and dehumanizing, where cruelty becomes a form of entertainment for the powerful.

*   **Frustration projection:** People often lash out at animals to relieve their own stress.
*   **The mockery culture:** Cruelty is sometimes performed for amusement by those in power.
*   **Lack of humor:** True humor is often suppressed, leading to dark, destructive behavior.

## The Father's Influence and Kindness

Mishi Khan attributes her deep compassion for animals to her upbringing, specifically the influence of her father. She describes him as having a "kind heart," a trait she strives to instill in her own children and followers.

She reflects on the irony of wasting food while animals go hungry, noting that many people treat animals like "side seats" in life, ignoring their suffering. Her philosophy is rooted in the belief that kindness is a divine gift that humans must extend to all living beings.

*   **Role modeling:** Parents must teach kindness to their children through action.
*   **Waste vs. Need:** Acknowledging the moral failure of wasting food while animals suffer.
*   **Empathy:** Understanding that animals feel pain and fear, just as humans do.

## Personal Hobbies and Shelter Work

Beyond her advocacy, Mishi Khan shares her personal passion for dogs. She enjoys driving to shelters to adopt dogs that cannot survive in their current environments, a hobby that has grown from a small shelter to housing sixty dogs.

She manages her shelter entirely through personal funds, refusing to commercialize it yet hoping to expand it in the future. She expresses immense dedication to the cause, admitting that relocating even a few hundred dogs would be overwhelming, but she remains committed to the work.

*   **Active adoption:** Driving to shelters to rescue dogs in need.
*   **Personal funding:** Running the shelter without commercialization.
*   **Future goals:** Expanding the shelter to include cats and more dogs.

## The Role of Media in Shaping Perception

The podcast touches upon how media representation influences public perception. Mishi Khan highlights the importance of showing the reality of animal suffering rather than just the "cute" side. She believes that exposing the harsh truths can spark necessary change and empathy.

She notes that while some people might be frustrated by the reality of animal abuse, the goal is to bring awareness to the masses. By sharing stories of cruelty, she hopes to shift the societal narrative from indifference to action.

*   **Reality over glamour:** Showing the true state of animal welfare.
*   **Storytelling:** Using narratives to evoke empathy and change behavior.
*   **Community impact:** Encouraging others to join the fight for animal rights.
