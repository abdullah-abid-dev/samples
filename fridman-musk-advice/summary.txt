Elon Musk advises young people to pursue careers and lives defined by tangible utility, aiming to contribute more to society than they consume rather than seeking leadership for its own sake. He recommends building broad general knowledge through extensive reading, such as skimming encyclopedias, to discover the intersection of one's talents and passions before specializing. Crucially, he urges individuals to adopt a "grow-the-pie" mindset instead of a zero-sum mentality, emphasizing that celebrating others and fostering collaboration expands opportunities for everyone rather than creating scarcity.
