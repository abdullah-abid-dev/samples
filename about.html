Transcription, Subtitles & Web Solutions

I'm Abdullah Abid, a web developer with expertise in Natural Language Processing (NLP). I provide accurate transcription and subtitle services that help make audio and video content more accessible and easier to engage with.

In addition to transcription and subtitling, I can also build custom web pages or enhance existing websites to publish transcripts alongside videos, helping make your content more accessible, searchable, and useful to a wider audience.

Services include:

  • Accurate audio and video transcription
  • Subtitle and caption generation
  • Transcript formatting and cleanup
  • Custom transcript pages for websites
  • Integration of transcripts with existing websites
  • NLP-powered solutions for audio and video content

My goal is simple: to help your content reach more people and make it easier for everyone to access, read, and engage with.
