Transcription & Content Accessibility

I'm Abdullah Abid — I turn audio and video into accurate, accessible transcripts and captions, and build the web pages that put them to work. With a background in web development and Natural Language Processing (NLP), I can take a conversation from recording all the way to a published, searchable, reusable content asset.

I've had the opportunity to work with people who are deaf or hard of hearing, providing transcripts and captions that let them access podcasts, interviews and other spoken content.

My core service is transcription & content accessibility. Web development is the part that publishes and presents the work — custom transcript pages, captions on your site, and integration with your existing platform — so the finished product is easy for your audience to read, search and reuse.

Services include:

  • Accurate audio and video transcription
  • Subtitle and caption generation (SRT / VTT)
  • Transcript formatting and cleanup
  • Speaker labelling and timestamp chapters
  • Content repurposing — show notes, blog posts, quotes
  • Web pages to publish and present your transcripts

My goal is simple: to help your content reach more people and make it easier for everyone to access, read, and engage with.
