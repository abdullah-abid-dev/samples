I'm Abdullah Abid — a Senior Backend Developer who builds robust web applications with Python and Django, and who also turns audio and video into accurate, accessible transcripts, captions and web pages.

Most of my day-to-day work is backend: RESTful APIs with Django and DRF, JWT authentication, asynchronous tasking with Celery, and DevOps (S3, Elastic Beanstalk, CodeBuild, CI/CD). I'm a strong advocate of Test-Driven Development and modular, decoupled design — the same care for accuracy and structure that I bring to a transcript.

Alongside the engineering, I care about making spoken content reachable. With a background that touches Natural Language Processing and web development, I take a conversation from a recording all the way to a published, searchable, reusable content asset — a transcript, captions, chapters, and a clean web page your audience can read along with.

What I do

  • Accurate audio and video transcription (speaker-labelled, timestamped)
  • Subtitle and caption generation (SRT / VTT)
  • Transcript formatting, cleanup and proofreading
  • Content repurposing — show notes, blog posts, quote graphics
  • Web pages to publish and present your transcripts
  • Backend & web development (Django, APIs, DevOps) for content platforms

Experience

  • Senior Backend Developer — Python/Django, DRF, Celery, cloud deployment & CI/CD (2023–present).
  • Backend Developer — from-scratch CRM web application, modular Django architecture (2019–2021).
  • Web Developer & Techno-Functional — WordPress sites, SAP B1, social media (2022–2023).

My goal is simple: reliable software and content that's easy for everyone to access, read, and reuse.
