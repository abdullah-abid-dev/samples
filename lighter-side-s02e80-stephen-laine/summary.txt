Host David Koffer introduces Stephen Lane, an autism advocate and father who shares his raw, unfiltered journey raising two sons with autism while navigating divorce and systemic challenges. Stephen recounts the difficult path of diagnosing his children, the financial barriers to therapy, and the profound trauma inflicted by school transitions and family separation. He emphasizes that advocacy often chooses parents rather than the other way around, highlighting how these experiences reshape families and underscore the critical need to address trauma alongside communication differences.
