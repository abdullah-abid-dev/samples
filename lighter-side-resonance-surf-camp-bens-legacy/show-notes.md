## Summary
A special edition of *The Lighter Side of the Spectrum* celebrates the Resonance Surf Camp, an inclusive initiative honoring the life of Kim Kredich's son, Ben. Host David Koffer joins Kim to reflect on a transformative three-day experience in Wrightsville Beach that brought families of all abilities together for surfing, dining, and connection. The episode highlights how the camp evolved from a personal family tradition into a community legacy, funded by donations from Ben's memory and supported by the Peyton Project to promote communication through spelling. Listeners will gain inspiration on how unique accommodations and genuine belonging can create ripple effects that foster inclusion in schools and beyond. This story proves that meaningful change doesn't require massive scale, but rather the simple act of planting seeds for connection and support.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Inclusion through Accommodation:** True inclusion in schools and communities requires specific supports, modifications, and accommodations tailored to individual needs.
*   **The Power of Spelling:** Using spelling boards (like the letterboard) has opened minds and hearts, allowing individuals to express desires and needs clearly.
*   **Adapting for Success:** Observing children in "loops" or repetitive movements offers opportunities to adjust teaching methods, such as starting surfers on their knees to build confidence.
*   **Community Belonging:** Shared experiences, from group dinners to beach activities, create a sense of belonging that is essential for the well-being of families.
*   **Legacy of Ben:** The Resonance Surf Camp serves as a lasting tribute to Ben, funded by community donations and designed to replicate the joy and support he loved.
*   **Small Steps Matter:** Meaningful inclusion can start with small, localized efforts like renting a movie theater or organizing a surf camp, planting seeds for future growth.
