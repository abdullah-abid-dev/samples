Privacy Policy

Last updated: this policy describes what information I collect, how it is used, and your rights. It is provided as a template — review it (and adapt it to your situation) before relying on it.

Information I collect

When you use the “Request a free sample” form, the following is sent to a third-party form processor (Formspree): your name, email address, any message you include, and an optional YouTube/video link. These are used only to reply to your enquiry and send the sample you requested.

How it is used

  • To respond to your enquiry and deliver requested sample materials.
  • To improve the site and the services offered. I do not sell or rent your information.

Third parties

The contact form is handled by Formspree, which stores submissions according to its own privacy policy. Analytics (if enabled) are provided by Plausible, a privacy-friendly, cookieless service that does not track individuals. No advertising cookies are used on this site.

Your files & confidentiality

Any audio, video or transcript you send is treated as confidential. Files are processed for your project and are not published or shared without your permission. Sample pages on this site are either publicly shared demos or, where marked, private reviewed samples accessed by password. Client deliverables are never published without explicit consent.

Your rights (GDPR / data protection)

If you are in the EU/UK, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights or to ask a question, email abdullah.abid.dev@gmail.com.
