## Summary
In this powerful episode of *The Lighter Side of the Spectrum*, host David Kaufer sits down with Angela Acres-Castro to explore the journey of a mother who trusted her instincts over medical dismissal. Angela shares how her son, Miles, was misdiagnosed as simply "slow" or "boyish" despite showing signs of autism as early as age five. By refusing to accept the narrative that boys mature slower, Angela persisted in seeking answers, eventually securing an autism diagnosis at age five and later an epilepsy diagnosis that explained his complex symptoms. The conversation highlights the critical importance of parental advocacy, the reality of limited services in Eastern Washington, and the profound impact of asking better questions. Angela's story serves as a reminder that while the future may differ from our dreams, the love and support of a family can create a life of connection and joy.

## Key Takeaways
*   **Trust Your Instincts:** Parents should not dismiss their gut feelings when their child's behavior doesn't fit the "typical" narrative, even if doctors suggest waiting.
*   **The Power of Advocacy:** Parents often become experts by necessity, navigating complex systems and challenging outdated stereotypes about autism in their communities.
*   **Beyond the Diagnosis:** An autism diagnosis can bring relief by validating a parent's experience and opening doors to necessary services and support.
*   **Individual Needs Vary:** Not all children with autism fit the stereotype of being antisocial or having severe dietary restrictions; every child is unique.
*   **Post-School Challenges:** The biggest hurdles for families often begin after high school graduation, requiring proactive research into adult support systems.
*   **Sensory and Behavioral Nuance:** Understanding specific triggers, such as sensory seeking behaviors or deep pressure needs, is crucial for effective care.
