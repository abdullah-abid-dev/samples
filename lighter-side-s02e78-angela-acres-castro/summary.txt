Host David Koffer welcomes Angela Akers Castro, a parent who became an advocate after her son, Miles, was misdiagnosed with autism at age five due to his atypical social affection and sensory needs. Angela recounts her journey of trusting her instincts against medical skepticism, eventually securing an autism and epilepsy diagnosis that explained Miles' behaviors, including his unique sensory seeking and food preferences. The conversation highlights the challenges families face post-school and Angela's subsequent efforts to create community support systems for adults with developmental disabilities in Washington State. Ultimately, the episode celebrates Miles' happy, lovable nature and emphasizes how parental advocacy can transform a child's life and inspire broader systemic change.
