How it works

From the moment you get in touch to the finished, published package — here is exactly what happens, and roughly when.

The process

1 · Send your file or link

Share a YouTube link, an audio/video file, or another accessible source. I confirm the source, deliverables and a delivery date up front — no surprises.

2 · Transcribe & diarize

I produce a speaker-labeled transcript with timestamps, then human-review it for accuracy: names, specialist terminology and sense — never blindly AI-generated.

3 · Draft deliverables

You receive SRT/VTT captions, TXT/DOCX transcripts and timestamp chapters, plus any add-ons you chose (show notes, blog post, quote graphics, analytics report).

4 · Review round

One free revision round: tweak wording, names, timestamps or format. I return the final, proofread package.

5 · Delivery & publish

Files are bundled and delivered, with an optional searchable web page so your audience can read along and reuse the content.

Timelines are indicative. Standard delivery is a few business days; rush options are available. I confirm a real date for your project before starting.
