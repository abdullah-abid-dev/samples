In this episode of *The Lighter Side of the Spectrum*, host David Kaufer welcomes Dr. Antigone Oreopoulos, a physiotherapist and advocate for motor-based communication among nonspeaking autistic individuals. Dr. Oreopoulos shares her personal journey of returning to clinical practice after her son's diagnosis, highlighting how her lived experience revealed the critical disconnect between an autistic person's communicative intention and their physical ability to execute it. She argues that current diagnostic frameworks often mislabel motor planning challenges as lack of comprehension, urging the medical community to recognize sensory-motor impairment as a core driver of social communication differences rather than a behavioral deficit. Ultimately, the conversation emphasizes the urgent need to bridge the gap between existing research and clinical action to better support neurodivergent families.
