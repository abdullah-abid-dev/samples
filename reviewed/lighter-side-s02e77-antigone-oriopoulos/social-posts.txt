1. Episode hook

What if someone understands exactly what you asked, and their body still cannot do it? That can look like refusal. It can be motor planning. New episode with physiotherapist Dr. Antigone Oreopoulos on The Lighter Side of the Spectrum.

2. Speller quote

“My brain understands, but it’s like my body is daydreaming.” Peyton Thorpe, read by Dr. Antigone Oreopoulos. The body is part of the conversation.

3. Tip

Autism tip of the week, from Dr. Antigone Oreopoulos: give more breaks than you think they need. If they cannot tell you, set a timer. And before you teach a motor skill, do it yourself and notice every step.

4. Testing

Putting a letterboard on a stand and saying “just point” skips the hard part. Pointing takes posture, motor planning, vision, and the ability to start and reverse a movement. Dr. Antigone Oreopoulos on why that matters for communication and for IQ testing.

5. Parents

“Know better, do better.” Antigone Oreopoulos on the month she spent realizing she had treated “can’t” as “won’t.” A parent confession on the latest episode of The Lighter Side of the Spectrum.
