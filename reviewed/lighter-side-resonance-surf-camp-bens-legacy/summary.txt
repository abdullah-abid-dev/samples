Host David Koffer and Kim Kretich reflect on a transformative three-day inclusive surf camp in Wrightsville Beach, originally conceived by Kim to support families of children with autism. The event, now renamed the Resonance Surf Camp in honor of Kim's late son Ben, successfully brought together diverse participants with varying abilities and communication needs, fostering a profound sense of belonging and community. Through shared experiences and innovative support strategies, the camp highlighted how inclusion and tailored accommodations can unlock potential and create lasting positive impacts.
