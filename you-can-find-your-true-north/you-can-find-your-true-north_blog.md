# You Can Find Your True North: Jordan B. Peterson's Path to True North

In today's podcast episode, Jordan B. Peterson discusses his work in helping individuals find their true north and the results of a groundbreaking clinical trial. Peterson, a renowned psychologist and author, shares insights on his process, the science behind it, and the impact it has had on participants.

## The True North Process

Peterson has spent nearly five decades working with people across the world, recognizing that while the brain is complex, the mind can be guided towards a clear sense of purpose. His approach involves helping individuals develop their true north, not by imposing values but by identifying what is most important to them at this stage of life. This process is designed to shift their focus towards what they truly want, rather than being driven by external conditioning.

### The Six-Day Process: Date with Destiny

One of the key processes Peterson uses is the "Date with Destiny" seminar, a six-day program aimed at helping participants determine their core values and align their actions with these values. Peterson emphasizes the importance of "pull motivation," where individuals are motivated by something they care about more than themselves, such as their family or a cause they believe in. This approach contrasts with "push motivation," which relies on willpower and external pressures.

### The Clinical Trial: A Breakthrough in Mental Health

Peterson's work was approached by two professors from Stanford University who were clinically depressed and had stopped taking medication. They participated in the "Date with Destiny" seminar and experienced significant improvements. This led to a clinical trial designed to test the effectiveness of Peterson's approach.

### The Study Results

The study, conducted over six weeks, involved a control group that received gratitude journaling and other interventions. The results were astonishing: 93% of participants showed no symptoms of depression after just the seminar. This outcome far exceeded the average improvement rates in conventional treatments, where only 40% of people show improvement, and 60% make no improvement.

### Long-Term Follow-Up

The study's long-term follow-up showed that participants experienced a 52% increase in positive emotions and a 71% decrease in negative emotions a year later. These results highlight the lasting impact of Peterson's approach on mental health and overall well-being.

## The Engagement Crisis: A Global Phenomenon

Peterson also discusses the current engagement crisis, particularly in the wake of the COVID-19 pandemic. The drop in engagement levels and the rise in active disengagement are concerning, as they can lead to decreased productivity and increased stress. Peterson's approach offers a potential solution by helping individuals regain a sense of control and purpose in their lives.

## The Future of Healthcare

Peterson's work challenges the one-size-fits-all approach to healthcare and emphasizes the importance of personalized care. Merrick Health, a platform that offers comprehensive lab work and personalized health coaching, is highlighted as an example of a service that aligns with Peterson's philosophy. Merrick Health's commitment to treating individuals as unique beings, with personalized protocols that evolve with them, sets it apart from traditional healthcare services.

## Conclusion

Jordan B. Peterson's work on helping individuals find their true north and the groundbreaking results of his clinical trial offer a promising solution to mental health and engagement issues. His approach, based on pull motivation and value alignment, has shown significant and lasting benefits. As the world continues to face challenges, Peterson's methods provide a beacon of hope for those seeking to regain control and purpose in their lives.

By understanding and applying the principles of true north, individuals can navigate the complexities of life with greater clarity and resilience. Whether through personal development seminars or comprehensive health platforms, the path to true north is one worth exploring.
